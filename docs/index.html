More than a budget

Investments, live markets, an economy panel, debt payoff, cash forecasts, retirement and a tax summary.

No subscription

Free to download and use. Optional automatic bank sync is a separate service you pay directly, about $15 a year.

How it stays free

No servers to pay for. Savings passed on to you.

Most finance apps charge a subscription because they run servers that store everyone's data. Larenest doesn't, so there's nothing to charge you for.

Your device does the work

Everything is stored and calculated on your own phone or computer. No cloud means no server bills, and no central database for anyone to breach.

Free data, straight from the source

Rates, inflation, energy prices and market data come from public sources that are free for personal use, like FRED, BLS, EIA, the U.S. Treasury, Finnhub and Financial Modeling Prep. You get your own free keys, and the app fetches only what you need.

Pay only for what you choose

Want automatic bank sync? It's optional, and you pay SimpleFIN directly, about $15 a year, with no markup from us.

No ads, no data deals

We don't make money from your information, so there's no reason to collect it. Privacy comes from how the app is built, not a policy promise.
