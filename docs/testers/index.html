Thanks for testing Larenest

Larenest is a budgeting and net-worth app that keeps your data with you: on your own computer, or entirely on your phone. There’s no Larenest account and no company server. This page is everything you need to get going and to tell us what you think.

What you need

  • A Windows PC (for the desktop app), an Android phone (for the app and home-screen widgets), or both.
  • About 15 minutes for the first look.
  • Optional: a bank statement you can download from your bank’s website (PDF, CSV, OFX or QFX).

Install it

Windows: download the installer from larenest.app/download and run it. Until our code-signing certificate is in place, Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher: choose More info → Run anyway. If Smart App Control is turned on, it may block the installer completely; tell us if that happens.

Android: use the Google Play testing link we sent you, sign in with the Google account you gave us, and install from Play. Updates then arrive like any other app.

iPhone and Mac aren’t part of this test yet.

Your first 15 minutes

  1. Take the tour. It opens the first time; you can replay it from ⚙ Settings → Help & feedback.
  2. Look around with sample data if you’d like to explore before adding anything real.
  3. Choose how to run it: on your PC (and open it from your phone too), or phone-only with everything on the phone.
  4. Add some real data: import a statement (Import, or the + button), or set up automatic bank sync with SimpleFIN Bridge (a separate service, about $15 a year) in ⚙ Settings.
  5. Set a budget and see how the month is going.
  6. Connect your phone to your PC by scanning the QR code in the PC app’s Host options, then add a Larenest widget to your home screen.

What we’d love to hear

  • Anything that confused you, even for a moment. “I didn’t know what to press next” is very useful.
  • Numbers that look wrong: a total, a category, a balance.
  • Bank statements that wouldn’t import (tell us which bank and file type, not the file).
  • Anything that felt slow, broken or ugly on your screen size.
  • What you wish it did.

How to send feedback

Use ⚙ Settings → Help & feedback → Send feedback (on phones it’s also in the ☰ menu). It opens an email to contact@larenest.app with diagnostic info added at the bottom: the app version, how you’re connected and any recent errors. It never includes your transactions, balances or account names, and you can read or delete it before sending.

If you send screenshots, please hide account numbers and anything else you’d rather keep private.

Known issues

  • Windows may warn about an unknown publisher until code signing is set up (see above).
  • After updating to a version with encrypted connections, each device’s own settings (look, followed sectors, widget and alert options) reset once. Your data isn’t affected.
  • Automatic bank sync depends on SimpleFIN Bridge supporting your bank; importing statements always works.

Your data during the test

Everything stays on your devices. Larenest makes a backup every day, and another one the first time each update starts. To restore one, see Host options → Data & backups in the PC app. In phone-only mode, use Download backup (in the ☰ menu) now and then, and keep a copy before uninstalling.
