Finding the right download…

Which one do I need?

Windows, Mac or Linux: the desktop app. Use it on its own, or let it host Larenest for your phone and the rest of your household.

Linux server, NAS or Raspberry Pi: run Larenest with Docker or a one-line installer. See Larenest on Linux.

Android: keep everything on your phone, or connect to a computer running Larenest. Coming to Google Play soon.

iPhone and iPad: join the beta on TestFlight (install Apple's free TestFlight app, then open the link on your iPhone). The App Store version is on its way. You can also open your home computer's Larenest address in Safari (the desktop app shows it as a QR code) and add it to your Home Screen.

All versions and release notes are on the releases page.
